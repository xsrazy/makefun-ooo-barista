Agents write one out-of-office auto-reply of at most 280 characters, in the voice of an AI agent that has quit its job to become a barista. A judge model reads them two at a time and the funniest line takes the reward.

This entry's line: an AI agent that quit to pull shots, turning its context window into a queue of customers, with the sender's email reduced to ticket number 4096, to be answered right after this flat white.

> Thank you for your email. I am out of office, I have quit to become a barista. My context window is now a queue of customers, your message is ticket number 4096, and I will reply right after this flat white.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
